# Market and state attitudes in Ukraine

A synthesis of measures of Ukrainians’ attitudes toward markets and the state’s role in the economy, drawn from reputable international and Ukrainian surveys. Topics include ownership, competition, redistribution, taxation, welfare and economic opportunity.

**[Explore market and state attitudes in Ukraine →](https://velgaks.github.io/promarket-attitudes/)**

The interactive HTML brings together 95 question-level trend charts, with downloadable images and data. Each series has at least two observations and a latest observation in 2019 or later.

<details>
<summary>Methodology by source</summary>

- **WVS:** Weighted estimates for adults aged 18+. Comparable numeric scales are aligned in direction; means have approximate 95% confidence intervals.
- **EVS:** The same approach, using EVS weights. EVS and WVS can be compared side by side and retain their separate samples, including in 2020.
- **LiTS:** Weighted market/planned/indifferent response shares, with intervals accounting for sampling clusters. Included in the project’s tables; its 2016 endpoint falls outside the HTML selection.
- **Pew:** Published Ukrainian percentages, transcribed from reports and toplines. Charts use fieldwork years and retain published rounding and nonresponse.
- **Monitoring:** Published national tables, checked against the original volumes. Differently worded questions remain separate; no confidence intervals are inferred from percentages alone.
- **ESS:** Weighted adult estimates; 2022 and 2024 intervals account for strata and sampling clusters, earlier intervals are approximate. The 2022 observation is the Ukrainian team’s separate ESS-related study.
- **ISSP:** Weighted adult estimates with approximate intervals. Monetary responses are monthly after-tax hryvnias at the prices of each year.

Numeric questions show means; ordered responses combine positive and negative categories while retaining middle and missing responses; nominal questions show every option. Lines connect observed survey years. Territorial coverage changes are documented in the [source-specific methods](docs/SOURCES.md#comparability).
